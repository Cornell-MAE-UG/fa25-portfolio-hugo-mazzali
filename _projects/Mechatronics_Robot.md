This competition challenge my knowledge of design and mechatronics. With each milestone, our team was taked to work together to apply the knowledge we gained during the course to solve real challenges. The time frame for the project also taught me how to work in a design group with a time crunch. Our robot ended up making it through 8 out of a possible 12 rounds which was satisfactory result. If I were to do this project again, I'd want to dedicate more time to testing the robot in real compeition conditions against other robots. This would help inform the team about things that need improvement that we may not have considered during our design process. Our robot ultimately lost because another robot was able to push it bakwards and take all the cubes it had collected. Had we identified this possibility during the design process, we could have implemented a method for keeping the cubes retained even in backwards movement or a gear reduction to ensure that our robot does not get pushed back.
