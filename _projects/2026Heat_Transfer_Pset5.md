 This problem set explored heat transfer through various material composites with different modes of heat transfer and geometry. 
 
 The first problem explored heat transfer through a rod with a uniform, volumetric, heat generation. For this problem, I conducted analysis using a differential control volume within the rod where I analyzied the heat transfer through an energy balance method. In the second part of this question, I developed the governing equations for the situation that described the temperature profile of the rod in the simplest manner, which I then completed by expressing the boundary conditions of the rod given initial and ambient temperatures as well as rod length. This problem was very useful for helping me develop an understanding as to how produce a systematic model for a heat transfer situation. Building the governing equation and boundary conditions for this situation helped me gain an understanding of heat transfer system modelling.

 The second problem was a 1-dimension heat conduction between surfaces. One surface had heat generation wand the other did not. The problem questions guided me through the thought process of developing a temperature profile plot across the two surfaces with different thermal properties. This problem was helpful in providing me with practice for developing temperature profiles across different materials.

 The third problem is the problem that I am most proud of, and that I feel provides me with the skills that best transfer to the work I want to be a part of as a mechanical engineer. Problem 3 examined a CTE situation where the efficiency of a gas turbine is improved by the application of a thermal barrier coating (TBC). I analysed the turbine operation efficiency with and without the TBC and established an efficiency improvement quantity by comparing the two operation conditions. I had to examine the heat transfer through the various materials of the turbine as well as how heat conduction affected the turbine's efficiency.
